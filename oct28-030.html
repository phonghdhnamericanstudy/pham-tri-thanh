01 / Research

Solar Tracker

During a charity trip to Con Cuong, Nghe An, I witnessed a harsh paradox: scorching 50°C Lao winds parched the land, yet rugged terrain left families without grid electricity. I installed solar panels and tracked output via a web dashboard, but found efficiency drops from solar blind spots while dual-panel setups were too costly and complex for locals. My "Eureka!" moment struck while watching village children spin wooden tops. Inspired by how conservation of angular momentum and the gyroscopic effect kept the top pivoting on a single tip, I engineered a fixed-axis Solar Tracker that follows the sun minimizing costs and maximizing power for highland homes.

ASO / Engineering drawings

From circuits to control panels.

During my internship at ASO Mechatronics, I supported the end-to-end design of power and control circuits, schematic diagrams, panel layouts, and comprehensive bill of materials (BOM) parts lists for industrial electrical panel projects. This hands-on experience deepened my expertise in reading complex electrical schematics, configuring soft starters and contactors, and applying rigorous engineering standards in a professional mechatronics environment.
